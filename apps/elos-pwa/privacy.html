Privacy Policy

Effective date: June 5, 2026  ·  Last updated: June 5, 2026

Elos ("we," "us," or "our") operates the Elos iOS application (the "App"). This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have. By using Elos you agree to these practices.

1. Information We Collect

Account & Profile Data

  • Email address and password (managed by Supabase Auth)
  • First and last name
  • Body metrics you choose to enter: height, weight, age
  • School name, school year, fitness experience level, and training goals

Fitness & Workout Data

  • Workout sessions: exercises performed, sets, reps, weight, RPE/RIR, timestamps
  • Workout templates and training splits you create
  • Personal records and progressive overload history
  • Daily readiness check-ins (sleep quality, soreness, stress, motivation)

Wellness Data

  • Sleep logs: bedtime, wake time, duration, quality rating
  • Daily hydration intake
  • Habit tracking data

Academic Data (Canvas LMS Integration — Optional)

  • If you choose to connect Canvas LMS, we access your Canvas courses, assignments, and exam dates using a personal access token you provide.
  • Your Canvas access token is stored securely in the iOS Keychain on your device and is never transmitted to our servers.
  • Canvas data is synced to your device for scheduling purposes only.

Social Data

  • Friend connections you create within the app
  • Leaderboard participation (total workout volume, visible to your friends)

Usage Data

  • Device type, operating system version, and app version (collected automatically by Supabase)
  • Error logs for debugging purposes

2. How We Use Your Information

  • Provide and personalize the Elos app experience
  • Calculate progressive overload suggestions, estimated 1RMs, and training analytics
  • Display your schedule by combining Canvas academic events with your training split
  • Show you leaderboard rankings among your friends
  • Authenticate your account and maintain session security
  • Diagnose technical issues and improve the app

We do not sell your personal data. We do not use your data for advertising.

3. How We Store Your Information

Your data is stored in two places:

  • On your device — using Apple SwiftData (local database). This includes cached workout history, habits, sleep logs, and Canvas data.
  • On our servers — using Supabase (PostgreSQL hosted on AWS). This includes your account, profile, workouts, social connections, and leaderboard data.

All data in transit is encrypted using TLS. Supabase enforces row-level security so you can only access your own data.

4. Sharing of Information

We share your information only in these limited circumstances:

  • Supabase — our backend database and authentication provider (supabase.com). Supabase processes your data on our behalf and is bound by a data processing agreement.
  • Friends within Elos — your display name and workout volume are visible to friends you connect with on the leaderboard.
  • Legal requirements — we may disclose information if required by law or to protect our rights and users' safety.

5. Data Retention

We retain your data for as long as your account is active. You can delete your account at any time from Settings → Delete Account within the app. Deleting your account permanently removes all your data from our servers within 30 days.

6. Children's Privacy

Elos is not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided us data, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.

7. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Correct inaccurate data
  • Delete your data (available in-app via Settings → Delete Account)
  • Export your data (contact us)

To exercise these rights or for any privacy questions, email us at support@elos.app.

8. Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your information, including TLS encryption in transit, Supabase row-level security, and iOS Keychain storage for sensitive tokens. No method of transmission over the Internet is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of material changes by updating the "Last updated" date above. Continued use of Elos after changes means you accept the updated policy.
